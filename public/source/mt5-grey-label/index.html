What Is an MT5 Grey Label?

Running a brokerage on MT5 normally means one of two things. Either you hold a licence and operate your own server, which is costly and slow to arrange, or you work with a company that already has that licence and operates the server for you.

The second option is a grey label. The licence holder runs the MT5 server. Your brokerage is given its own section of it, with your brand on the client side and a manager account you use to look after your traders. Groups, leverage, spreads, account balances and reports all sit under your control.

Your traders never deal with the arrangement behind the scenes. They download MT5, find your brokerage, log in and trade.

02 / 13

Why MT5 Rather Than MT4?

MT5 was built as a separate platform, not an update to MT4, and the differences show up in daily trading.

It handles a broader range of markets, including exchange-traded instruments as well as forex and CFDs, so it suits brokers planning a wider product list. Charts come with 21 timeframes instead of 9. Traders get six pending order types, including stop limit orders. Accounts can run in hedging or netting mode. There's a built-in economic calendar, depth of market and a faster strategy tester.

One thing to weigh up: MT5 expert advisors are written in MQL5, and MT4 tools don't carry across automatically. If a large part of your audience relies on MT4 EAs, running both platforms is often the answer.

How It Works

Think of it as a relay.

The licence holder keeps the MT5 server running, handles updates and carries the obligations tied to the licence. That's the first leg.

Your manager account takes the baton. From there, your team configures groups and symbols, sets trading conditions and watches client activity. Your section of the server is kept apart from other brokers using it.

Your systems run the final leg. A trader registers on your website, gets approved in your CRM and has an MT5 account opened automatically through the manager API. Deposits arrive through your payment providers and update their balance. Orders route through your execution setup, whether that's an arrangement through the licence holder or your own liquidity connection.

When each leg knows its job, handoffs stay smooth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an MT5 grey label provider do?+

A provider arranges your access on a licensed MT5 server, configures your manager account and branding, and connects MT5 to your CRM and other systems.

Is MetaTrader 5 grey label available for new brokers?+

Yes, through licence holders, subject to their terms and the vendor's current rules. We confirm availability in writing.

Can I run MT4 and MT5 together?+

Yes. Many brokers offer both, managed from the same CRM.

Will MT4 expert advisors work on MT5?+

Not directly. MT5 uses MQL5, so EAs need an MT5 version or rewriting.

Can I choose hedging or netting accounts?+

MT5 supports both. What's available on your setup depends on the arrangement.

Are you affiliated with MetaQuotes?+

No. We work with licence-holding partners and are not endorsed by MetaQuotes.

Do I need a financial licence?+

It depends on your business model and where your clients are. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Can I add PAMM or MAM later?+

Yes. Plugins can be added once your MT5 grey label is running.
