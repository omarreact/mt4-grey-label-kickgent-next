What Is an MT4 Grey Label?

Picture a shopping centre. The owner holds the lease on the whole building, maintains it and keeps the lights on. Individual shops rent space inside, put their own name over the door and run their business however they choose.

An MT4 grey label works in much the same way. A company that holds an MT4 licence runs the server. Your brokerage gets its own space on that server, with your brand facing your clients and a manager account that lets you run your side of things. You decide who your traders are, which groups they sit in, what leverage they get and how your spreads are set.

This matters more for MT4 than for almost any other platform. The vendor no longer issues new MT4 licences. If you want to offer MetaTrader 4 grey label access today, working through an existing licence holder is the realistic way in.

Who Needs an MT4 Grey Label?

This suits brokers whose audience is already comfortable on MT4:

  • Founders targeting traders who have used MT4 for years and don't want to switch
  • IBs who want their clients on a brand they own instead of someone else's
  • Brokers serving expert advisor users, since MT4 EAs don't run on MT5
  • Businesses testing a new region before committing to larger infrastructure
  • Brokers already on MT5 who keep losing prospects that only want MT4

If your plans depend from day one on running the server yourself, a grey label won't give you that, and we'll say so.

How It Works

Three layers sit on top of each other.

The licence holder runs the bottom layer: the MT4 server itself, its maintenance, updates and the licence that keeps it legitimate.

You run the middle layer through your manager account. That's where your clients live, along with their groups, trading conditions and account balances. Your section is kept separate from any other broker on the same server.

The top layer is everything your traders touch. Your website, your client portal, your onboarding emails and the MT4 terminal they log in to. When someone registers and gets approved in your CRM, the CRM reaches through the manager API and opens their MT4 account. When they deposit, the balance updates. When they trade, the order follows your execution setup.

Knowing which layer owns what makes it far easier to solve problems quickly.

Security, Reliability and Support

The licence holder looks after server maintenance, monitoring and updates. On your side, security depends largely on how manager access is handled. Give each person only the permissions their job needs. Someone answering client emails doesn't need the ability to change leverage or apply credits.

Two-step verification on CRM logins, regular access reviews and removing accounts promptly when people leave are simple steps that prevent a lot of trouble.

After launch, we're available for configuration changes, technical issues and new integrations as your brokerage grows.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an MT4 grey label provider actually do?+

A provider arranges your access on a licensed MT4 server, sets up your manager account and branding, and connects the platform to your CRM and other systems.

Is MetaTrader 4 grey label still available?+

Yes, through companies that hold existing MT4 licences. Terms depend on the licence holder, and we confirm them before you commit.

Are you connected to MetaQuotes?+

No. MetaQuotes develops MetaTrader 4. We work with licence-holding partners and are not affiliated with or endorsed by MetaQuotes.

Can I control leverage and spreads?+

Yes. Within your groups, leverage and spreads are set by you.

Will my clients see my brand?+

Yes, on the client side. How far branding extends depends on the arrangement, and we'll show you exactly what traders see.

Can I add PAMM or MAM tools?+

Yes. PAMM and MAM plugins can be added so money managers can trade for multiple investors.

Do I need a licence to run my brokerage?+

That depends on your model and where your clients are. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Can I add MT5 later?+

Yes. Many brokers start on MT4 and add an MT5 grey label as their client base grows.
