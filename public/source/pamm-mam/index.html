What Is a PAMM MAM Plugin?

Say a trader on your platform has a strong track record, and twenty people want to put money behind their strategy. Without the right tools, the options are messy. Investors could share logins, which is risky. The trader could place the same trade twenty times, which is impractical. Or someone could split results by hand at the end of each month, which invites errors.

A PAMM MAM plugin solves this properly. It sits on top of MT4 or MT5 and lets one manager handle many investors at once. The plugin works out who owns what, applies each trade fairly, charges fees under agreed rules and reports results back to everyone.

02 / 13

PAMM and MAM, Explained Simply

PAMM: A Shared Pot

With PAMM, investors' money goes into one shared pot. The manager trades the pot as a whole. When the pot gains or loses, each investor's slice moves by the same percentage, because their share was set by how much they contributed.

It's easy for investors to follow and requires nothing from them except choosing a manager. It's a common choice for MT4 PAMM and MT5 PAMM offerings aimed at passive investors.

MAM: One Conductor, Many Instruments

With MAM, every investor keeps a separate trading account. The manager places a trade once, and the plugin copies it across all connected accounts using a chosen allocation method, such as account equity, balance, fixed lots or a set percentage.

Because accounts stay distinct, the manager can treat them a little differently where needed. A MAM plugin for MT4 or MT5 suits managers looking after clients with different account sizes or preferences.

How It Works

The plugin runs alongside your MT4 or MT5 server and communicates through the manager API.

A manager is set up with a master account and their offering, whether PAMM or MAM, along with fee terms. Investors find the offering in your client portal, join and fund their participation through the CRM.

When the manager trades, the plugin applies the results to each investor according to the model. Fees are calculated at the agreed intervals. Investors see their balance, returns and fees in their dashboard. When someone wants to withdraw, the request goes through your usual CRM approval flow.

Your brokerage stays in control throughout, with full records of every allocation and fee.

Regulation

When someone trades money that belongs to other people, regulators often take an interest. Rules for managed-account products differ widely, and may apply to the broker, the manager or both.

Regulatory requirements for managed-account products vary by jurisdiction. Get legal advice for your markets before launching a PAMM or MAM offering. We supply the technology, not regulatory permission.

Security and Support

Allocation rules and fee settings directly affect investors' money, so access to change them should be limited and logged. Managers should only see their own investors, and investors only their own accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the plugin work on both MT4 and MT5?+

PAMM and MAM tools are available for both platforms, depending on the plugin and your arrangement.

What's the main difference between PAMM and MAM?+

PAMM pools funds and shares results by percentage. MAM keeps separate accounts and copies trades across them.

Can managers charge performance fees?+

Yes. Performance fees, management fees and high-water marks can usually be configured.

How do investors join a manager?+

Through your client portal, with funding handled by your CRM and payment providers.

Can investors withdraw at any time?+

Withdrawal terms are set by you and the manager, and processed through your CRM approval flow.

Do I need a licence to offer managed accounts?+

It depends on your jurisdiction and where your clients are. Regulatory requirements for managed-account products vary.
