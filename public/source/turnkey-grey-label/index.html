What a Turnkey Grey Label Brokerage Is

Building a brokerage from separate parts is a bit like renovating a house with a different contractor for every room. Each might do good work, but somebody has to make sure the plumbing meets the wiring, and that somebody is usually you.

A turnkey forex brokerage takes that coordination off your plate. We plan, configure and connect the core technology as a single project, starting from an MT4 or MT5 grey label and building outward. When it's finished, you have a system where a trader can register, verify, deposit, trade and withdraw, without staff patching gaps by hand.

What turnkey doesn't cover are the parts that depend on outside decisions: forming your company, getting any licence you need and securing approval from payment providers. We'll guide you on those, and we'll be clear about where our work ends and theirs begins.

The End-to-End Stack

Every turnkey build is tailored, and inclusions are confirmed in writing. A typical stack is made up of these layers.

Trading Layer

  • MT4 grey label, MT5 grey label or both
  • Manager access for groups, leverage, spreads and account control
  • Branding on the client side of the platform
  • Desktop, web and mobile access for traders

Business Layer

  • Forex broker CRM with lead management, KYC workflow and reporting
  • Client portal for registration, verification, deposits, withdrawals and transfers
  • IB area for partners and commission tracking
  • Automatic account creation through the manager API

Execution Layer

  • Liquidity bridge connection to your chosen providers, where your grey label arrangement allows
  • Routing rules for A-book, B-book or hybrid models
  • Failover and execution logging

Front-End Layer

  • Broker website with account pages, platform downloads and legal pages
  • Registration and login linked to the CRM

Infrastructure Layer

  • Hosting for the CRM, website and supporting tools
  • Monitoring and backups as agreed

Optional Add-Ons

  • PAMM and MAM plugins for money managers
  • Extra languages for website and portal
  • Additional payment provider integrations
  • Prop firm tools

How the Stack Works as One

Here's what happens once it's all connected.

Your website captures a new registration and passes it to the CRM. The trader verifies through the client portal. Compliance approves them in the admin panel. The CRM opens their MT4 or MT5 account through the manager API and emails their details. They deposit through an integrated payment provider, and their balance updates automatically. When they trade, orders flow through your bridge or the grey label execution arrangement. Every step is logged for your team.

None of those handoffs are left for you to wire together.

Implementation Roadmap

Milestone 1: Blueprint

We gather what we need to design your setup: target clients and markets, platform choice, account types, execution model, IB structure, brand assets and any providers you already have. We also check your company, licensing and payment status, since these affect timing.

Milestone 2: Agreement

Package contents, add-ons, responsibilities and timeline are set out in writing. Grey label terms are confirmed with the licence holder.

Milestone 3: Platform Ready

Your MT4 or MT5 manager account is configured with groups, symbols, leverage, spreads and branding.

Milestone 4: Back Office Ready

The CRM, client portal and IB area are configured and connected to the platform.

Milestone 5: Execution Connected

Bridge and liquidity connections are set up and tested, or the grey label execution route is confirmed.

Milestone 6: Front End Live in Staging

Your website is built and linked to registration and login, ready for testing.

Milestone 7: Payments Integrated

Providers you've been approved by are connected to the CRM.

Milestone 8: Full Journey Test

Test traders go from first click to withdrawal. Partners check their dashboards. Every payment method and email is checked.

Milestone 9: Handover and Launch

Your team is trained, systems go live and support continues from there.

What Stays With You

To avoid surprises, these sit outside the turnkey technology scope:

  • Forming and maintaining your company
  • Licensing decisions and applications
  • Payment provider and banking relationships
  • Running compliance day to day
  • Marketing and attracting clients
  • Client service and dealing decisions

We can point you in the right direction on many of these, but the decisions and approvals are yours or belong to the relevant authorities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a turnkey forex brokerage include?+

Typically an MT4 or MT5 grey label, a CRM with client portal and IB area, liquidity connections where allowed, a broker website and hosting, all connected and tested.

Does turnkey include a licence?+

No. Licensing depends on your business and markets, and approval sits with regulators. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Who connects payment providers?+

We handle the technical integration once you're approved. Applying to providers and maintaining the relationship sits with you.

Can I choose MT4, MT5 or both?+

Yes. Either platform, or both, can form the trading layer.

Can I add PAMM or MAM later?+

Yes. Plugins can be added after launch.

How is pricing set?+

It depends on your platform choice, CRM scope, add-ons and integrations. Quotes are based on your actual configuration.
