FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you build

Platform availability, integration scope and operational ownership should be understood before implementation begins.

Can I launch with both MT4 and MT5?+

Yes. Where the required platform arrangements are available, both can be connected to the same wider CRM and brokerage workflow.

Does the CRM connect to trading accounts automatically?+

A connected implementation can create and manage trading accounts through the relevant manager APIs instead of relying on repeated manual entry.

Can I add PAMM or MAM after launch?+

Yes. The stack is designed so additional products can be introduced later when the brokerage is ready operationally and legally.

Do you provide investment or brokerage services?+

No. GreyLabel Technology is positioned as a technology provider. Regulatory, licensing and investment decisions remain with the operating business and its advisers.
